PROMPT TO ENTER INTO CRAFTIFAI PIPEGEN (STAGE 1):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Build an edge AI computer vision pipeline for industrial worker safety named
SafetySentinel AI.

The pipeline monitors an industrial video feed (USB camera, RTSP stream, or
local surveillance MP4) at 1080p resolution.

Process workflow:
1. Ingest camera video frames through nvstreammux at 1920x1080 resolution, 30 FPS.
2. Run an object detection model compiled into a high-performance TensorRT engine
   (optimized with FP16 and INT8 quantization) to detect 'person', 'hardhat',
   and 'no-hardhat' with high inference throughput (>60 FPS).
3. Assign consistent tracking IDs across frames using nvtracker (NvDCF tracker).
4. Apply a custom safety assessment probe to associate detected helmets with
   detected workers:
   a. Compute spatial intersection between the upper head-region of each worker
      bounding box and detected helmet bounding boxes.
   b. If a worker has no associated helmet detected, flag a potential helmet violation.
   c. If a worker enters a designated restricted hazard zone, flag an immediate zone breach.
5. Annotate video frames with color-coded bounding boxes and labels via nvdsosd.
6. Generate actionable safety outputs beyond screen displays:
   a. Write structured JSON incident records to '/home/incidents.jsonl' containing
      timestamp, camera_id, worker_id, event_type, confidence, and bounding box.
   b. Publish real-time alert events to MQTT broker on topic 'workplace/safety/alerts'.
   c. Stream system performance telemetry (FPS, inference latency) to drive an
      external operations dashboard."
